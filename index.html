How the week works

  1. On Match week, pick the fixture. The match card is already filled in from the fixture list.
  2. Choose the 15 starters, the replacements and staff, or paste the team sheet in.
  3. Press Save all four cards and post them to Instagram as one carousel.
  4. Anything you change on the Squad, Fixtures or Setup tabs is saved on this computer only. Use the box below to send those changes to David so they can be added for good.
